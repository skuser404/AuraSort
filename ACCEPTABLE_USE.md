# Rules of use (Acceptable Use)

Version: 1.0.0
Effective date: 10 November 2026
Owner: Sunil Kumar (GitHub: skuser404)

> **Note:** Template provided for convenience, not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review before relying on it.

> **The short version**
> - Only touch files you own or are allowed to manage.
> - Do not use AuraSort to snoop on, collect or expose other people's information.
> - Do not weaken its protections or expose it to a network.
> - Use the AI features responsibly, and prefer the local mode for sensitive documents.

## Your files only
- Only scan, move, copy or delete files you own or are authorised to manage.
- Never use AuraSort on someone else's computer, a shared drive, or customer or employer data without permission.

## Other people's information
- Do not use AuraSort to find, collect, copy or expose other people's personal, financial or confidential information.

## Protections and the network
- Do not remove, bypass or weaken the built-in protections: project and system folder protection, view-only types,
  confirmations, the localhost-only binding and the per-run token.
- Do not expose the AuraSort server to a network: no port forwarding, tunnels or reverse proxies.

## Harmful use
- Do not use AuraSort for malware, unauthorised access, stalking or harassment, evidence tampering, or hiding or
  destroying data to avoid a legal or contractual duty.

## Copyright and licences
- Respect the copyright and licences of the files you handle.

## AI features
- Respect your organisation's data-handling rules before using the AI features on work documents. Prefer the local mode
  for banking, job, legal and medical documents.
- Do not send documents flagged Confidential to a cloud AI provider unless you have the right to do so.

## Asking for help
- Do not share screenshots, reports, CSV exports or logs that contain private file names or paths when asking for help or
  opening GitHub issues.

## Laws
- Follow the laws that apply to you, including where they apply the Information Technology Act, 2000 (India), the
  Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the GDPR (EU and UK) and similar local laws.
- AuraSort itself does not collect personal data. Responsibility for the lawful use of your files and of any AI provider
  you connect lies with you.
