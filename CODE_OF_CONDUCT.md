# Code of Conduct

Version: 1.0.0
Effective date: 10 November 2026
Owner: Sunil Kumar (GitHub: skuser404)

> **Note:** Template provided for convenience, not legal advice. Have a qualified lawyer review before relying on it.

> **The short version**
> - Be kind, be patient, assume good intent.
> - No harassment, insults or sharing other people's private information.
> - Report problems privately to the maintainer; reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0).

## Our pledge
We want taking part in AuraSort to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation. We act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour
- Show empathy and kindness, and respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; take responsibility and apologise for mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use AuraSort.
- Protect privacy: never post personal files, full paths with user names, API keys or screenshots of private documents.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or e-mail address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
The maintainer (Sunil Kumar, GitHub: skuser404) clarifies and enforces these standards and may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow them. Report problems privately through
[GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/skuser404/AuraSort/security/advisories/new) (mark the report as a
conduct report) or by contacting the maintainer on GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially.

Possible responses, depending on the impact: a private written warning, a temporary ban from interaction, or a permanent
ban from the project's spaces.

## Scope
This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in
public.
